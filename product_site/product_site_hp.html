What Is PickEatz?

Have you ever wanted to eat dinner with friends or family and no one can decide on what to eat? Someone is allergic to peanuts, another person won't eat fish, and you don't want to have to cook multiple meals or go to different restaurants. That's where PickEatz comes in! We take into account everyones eating preferences and dietary restrictions to offer you the best food options that cater to everyone. Whether it's a restaurant or a meal at home, we will do our best to make sure you and your friends and family can enjoy a meal all together.

Here are some of the features in the app:

  • "Family" System
  • Recipe/Restaurant Recommendations
  • Ingredient Shopping
  • Roulette Wheel

We hope to collaborate with certain food partners and companies like grocery stores, certain restaurants and food delivery services like DoorDash and UberEats. If you would like to contact us with any questions or business inquiries, please visit the CONTACT page or email us here.
