Want to learn how to use PickEatz? Look no further!

We made sure that PickEatz would be as simple to use as possible, because we want to make the dining experience easy and enjoyable for out users. After reading through this page anyone should be able to get the hang of the app and use it's basic features without a problem. App Tutorial:

Make sure to sign up for an account with an email and password! Next, make a profile for yourself, selecting your eating preferneces and dietary restrictions. Now, make profiles for people you want to use the app with (friends/family), and create a group and name it. Then press the 'DECIDE EATZ' button. Finally, look through recipes/restaurants that cater to all the people in your group and enjoy a meal together!
