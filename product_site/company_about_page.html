About Our Company

PickEatz is a development company that aims to make life easier for people all around the world. We develop apps and products targeting difficulties in the lifestyle of the average person. Our main focus now is on encouraging connection with others, especially through food. That's where PickEatz came from.

How PickEatz Started

In 2023, our president Woowop had been eating dinner at his dining room table with his family. Everyone was on their phones, including him, and he even realized they weren't eating the same food. He had some Chick-fil-a, his mother had some Popeyes and his dad was eating a HotPocket. Something was terribly wrong.

The next day he met up with our executive officer, Nehemiah Speaks and some other friends for lunch. Someone was in the mood for italian food, Woowop wanted something light and refreshing, another friend couldn't have anything with dairy because they were lactose. Everyone argued in the car until they were all just upset with each other. Eventually they had to just drive to different places and spend a lot of money on gas. After this hangout Woowop had enough. He reached out to Nehemiah with an idea to make eating a pleasant social experience that's easy for everyone, no matter what their dietary restrictions or preferences are. We eventually ended up with PickEatz in 2026.

Our Company's Values

As a company, PickEatz values collaboration, diversity, and innovation. Collaboration and working with others leads to unknown greatness. You have no idea what someone else can be thinking and what their idea can do for the world, so put your ideas together and make something great. Diversity is also important to use because you can't live in an echo chamber. We need to hear what people from different walks of life have experienced to encourage new ideas and help all kinds of people. Without diversity, the world would only cater to one type of person. Lastly, innovation leads to improvements in all facets of life. We innovate and improve the world around us with every step. What's your next step?

Our Company's Mission

Here at PickEatz, we strive to make life enjoyable with friends and family. We aim to lessen the stress that comes with picking something to eat for everyone and want you to focus on enjoying the meal as a group. Food is a way to connect with others. If you can share a meal with someone, you should be able to share ideas and feelings. We want you to focus on that aspect, so let us handle the tedious task of picking something to eat.
